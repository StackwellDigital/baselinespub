The Vibe

More Than Just a Pub

Baselines Pub is your go-to spot in South Surrey for drinks, games, and good times. We serve delicious food from the Sandcastle Grill kitchen right to your table — burgers, wings, pizza, and more.

Whether you're here for a casual beer after work, shooting pool with friends, or catching the big game on one of our many screens, Baselines is where everyone's welcome.
